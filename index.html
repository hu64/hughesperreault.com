Hughes Perreault - AI researcher

About

Hi! I'm Hughes, an AI researcher at the Institut de recherche d'Hydro-Québec (IREQ), where I apply machine learning to problems in the energy sector. Before that, I was Principal AI Developer at Haylem and an AI Scientist at Thales, where I worked on computer vision at the edge, including multimodal image fusion, thermal and visible image registration, and lightweight target detection. During my Ph.D. at Polytechnique Montréal, I focused on object detection and scene understanding for transportation, and achieved noteworthy results on a variety of road user datasets, such as Cityscapes, KITTI, India Driving, UA-DETRAC, and UAVDT. I have extensive experience with deep learning tools such as TensorFlow and PyTorch, as well as classic computer vision.
