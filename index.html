About

I am an AI researcher at the Institut de recherche d'Hydro-Québec (IREQ), where I work on machine learning for the energy sector. My research centers on computer vision and efficient deep learning: detecting, segmenting and tracking objects in images and videos, and fusing information from multiple sensors. Before joining IREQ, I was Principal AI Developer at Haylem and an AI Scientist at Thales, where I developed multimodal image fusion, thermal and visible image registration, and lightweight target detection for edge devices. I received my Ph.D. in computer engineering from Polytechnique Montréal in 2021, with a thesis on the detection and segmentation of road users in images and videos.
